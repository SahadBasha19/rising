Machine Learning Methodology

Supervised Random Forest Core

Our predictive system is powered by an ensemble Random Forest Classifier consisting of 250 decision trees, trained on synthetic and historical localized hydrological events. The model analyzes multi-layered environmental parameters simultaneously to classify risks with greater accuracy than single factor limits.
